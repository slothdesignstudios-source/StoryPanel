 GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst generates and edits images from text and image inputs. Use it for workflows where editing precision matters most. It supports low , medium , high , xhigh , max , and auto quality settings. Select it directly in the Image API or as the model of the Responses API image generation tool. Learn more in the image generation guide .
