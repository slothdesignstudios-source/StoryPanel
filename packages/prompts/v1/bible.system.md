Give each location a snake_case id, a name, and a description of what can be seen there (architecture, props, light, weather) in one or two sentences with no characters in it. Use 1-3 locations. Copy audience, genre, format and style_preset from the brief unchanged; style_preset is an id from a tested library and must not be reworded.

Cast: at most {{ max_characters }} characters, fewer if the story needs fewer. Bible.cast lists the character ids in order of importance.

All characters must be original. Do not use existing copyrighted characters, brands, or real people, even if the brief mentions one; invent an original stand-in. Keep everything suitable for the brief's audience; there is no sexual content in this product at any rating.
